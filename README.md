# Mobile-Sales-Analysis-
The objective of this dashboard is to analyse and monitor mobile phone sales performance across different brands, models, locations, payment methods, and time periods in order to support data-driven business decisions

**DATASET**
Let me give you a quick overview of the dataset, The dataset contains 3,835 sales transactions collected over 3 years from October 2021 to October 2024. 
It includes 14 key columns covering: transactions details - Date, transactions ID, Quantity sold ,
Product information - Brand, mobile model, price per unit ,
Customer data - Name, age, city
Payment information - Payment method used.
The data covers 5 major brands: Apple, Samsung, OnePlus, Vivo, and Xiaomi, with 14 different mobile models being tracked. 

**SCOPE**
The scope of this Mobile Sales Dashboard is to provide a complete 360-degree view of mobile sales performance across brands, models, cities, and time periods.
It helps in monitoring key performance indicators like total sales, quantity sold, total transactions, and average price in real time.
